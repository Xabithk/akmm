Learning that
shapes the whole.

At Ahmad Kutty Musliyar Memorial College of Islamic & Arts, students combine Higher Secondary education with Islamic Studies and continue onward to degree studies.

The college is located in Kainikkara, near Tirur, and is affiliated with the Samastha National Education Council (SNEC). This website introduces the institution and its academic direction; further campus details will be published when confirmed by the college.
